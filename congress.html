How to save a contact

Tap Add to contacts next to each official, then follow the steps for your phone.

iPhone

  1. Tap Add to contacts. The contact card opens.
  2. Scroll down and tap Create New Contact.
  3. A New Contact screen opens. Tap the check mark at the top right.
  4. You're back on the contact card. Tap the check mark at the top left to return to this page.

If the New Contact screen opens straight away, start at step 3.

Android

  1. Tap Add to contacts. When the card has downloaded, tap Open.
  2. If you're asked which app to use, choose Contacts, then Just once or Always. Always skips this question next time.
  3. Contacts shows the card. Tap Save contact at the bottom.

If the Open button goes away, open your Files or Downloads app and tap the card's file, which is named after the official (for example Patty-Murray.vcf). Then choose Contacts.

On a computer

The card downloads as a file. To add it to Google Contacts, follow Google's import steps (opens in a new tab). It's quicker to do on your phone.
